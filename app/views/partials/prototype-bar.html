About this prototype

Your Support — GOV.UK Prototype Kit migration. Built from the reviewed high-fidelity wireframes.

What this is

Two journeys, selected from the Start buttons on the bar below.

1. Request support — 16 screens, from the start page through to confirmation.

2. Manage support — the Employment Tribunals case overview, Reasonable adjustments page, and a confirmation step. Early.

No data leaves this prototype. Answers are held in the browser session until you select Reset or clear prototype data.

Important limitations

  • Special measures are out of this iteration.
  • Manage support uses fixed example data and does not submit or change anything.
  • The are-you-sure screen has no wireframe and is based on a verbal description.
  • Add a new support request on Reasonable adjustments does nothing because its destination is undecided.
  • The sign-language list is placeholder data, not reference data.
  • Sign out, Cymraeg, feedback, contact and footer links are shown for fidelity but are inert.

Known inconsistencies are deliberate

The source wireframes contain wording, character-limit and naming inconsistencies. They remain visible so they can be reviewed rather than silently resolved in the prototype.

This demonstration is configured as one service

The service tokens are filled as Employment Tribunals, party John Doe, tribunal and claim. Unresolved contact tokens remain in brackets.
